How to Play
1

Scoop letter tiles out of the junk pile and drag them onto the conveyor belt to spell a word.

2

Reorder tiles on the belt by dragging, or drop one back into the pile to swap it out.

3

Hit Weld when you've got a real word. A valid weld clears one of your targets and scores points. A bad weld fizzles and scatters the tiles back into the pile — costing you Integrity.

4

Run out of Integrity and the haul ends. Clear every target to advance — the pile gets bigger and trickier each level.

5

Stuck? Use a Magnet to pull a useful tile straight out of the pile.
